## Neverwinter: The Jewel of the North

**Region:** Sword Coast, Northwest Faerûn  
**Government:** Lord Protector rulership (current: Lord Dagult Neverember)  
**Population:** ~23,000 (mostly humans, with dwarves, half-elves, and halflings)  
**Primary Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Elvish  
**Notable Factions:** Lords’ Alliance, Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, Many-Arrows orcs (nearby), Zhentarim (covert)
 
Neverwinter is a major city on the Sword Coast, known for its mild climate, sophisticated architecture, and magical craftsmanship. Once devastated by the eruption of Mount Hotenow in 1451 DR, the city is now in a period of rapid reconstruction and transformation under the rule of **Dagult Neverember**, former Open Lord of Waterdeep.  
By 1489 DR, the city is a patchwork of old grandeur, new construction, and unstable ruins. Though Neverember has brought stability and security, many see his rule as opportunistic and authoritarian. Tensions simmer between **loyalists of the old Alagondar dynasty**, native citizens, refugees, and outside factions.
