After making it through gate an invisible guard appears and asks for a bribe in the form of Sarith's dagger, which they give him.
LEVEL UP

SHOPPING, got some raise dead diamonds, upgraded holy symbol, got new armor. 

During their stay Mako and Krankle see some drow women looking around the inn and follow them invisibly, overheard a little of them talking about Ilvara (the drow prison leader). Basil and Saz meet Duagloth.

Krankle ditches Mako who chills in town and a stone giant ==with two heads== attacks the town. She banishes it until the group arrives, they hold actions to attack at once, she path to the grave kills him.

A second stone giant appears (named Doorhoon) and explains that was his friend who went crazy. Revivified the giant (spend small diamond), still crazy to one HP, Duagloth kills him.

The giant tells them to go to Cairngorm Cavern and meet with the Stone Speaker, the giant leader(?)

The group meets with the city guard Captain Blackskull, who is generally reasonable and gives them a visa to go to the main city area in return wants "juicy secrets" about either the Grayghosts (thieve's guild) or Droki, a thief with them.
