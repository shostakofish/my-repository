"Foolish to pray to a false god. A god makes himself known at long last. Diinkarazan LIVES"

> Diinkarazan: Known as "the mad god" twin brother of Diirinka (Vengeance)
> Diirinka: patron god of derro, (Cruelty, knowledge, magic)

Bupido and Shushar are gone. Bupido almost certainly guy who killed other prisoners. Bupido seen leading shushar away at knifepoint. 

While investigating the group saw a ritual begin with a Duagar sacrifice (Hemeth) tied up on the deep father's altar, Mako pretended to be brought in by glooglagog (the son of the previous priestess) and the remaining members hidden in the crowd. 

The deep father priestess and his sister were heavily damaged and killed off first round via path to the grave+sneak attack. Her blood runs into the altar and **the demogorgon** is summoned. 

It attacks the city killing tons and doing huge damage to buildings. The group saves their fellow prison escapees and the Duagar sacrifice, escaping on their boat via control water. Fendrick is hit by rubble and fails a wisdom save to be taken over by madness, permanently gaining the mentality "if you dont agree with me, I'll beat you into submission" He attacks Hemeth on the boat to kill him, attacks the group, and is killed.

Traveled on foot after boat touched land on the way to Grecklestug. Met up with Julian's new character (Szazzorahc Blund'varr) being attacked by two wererats  (topsy and turvy)
