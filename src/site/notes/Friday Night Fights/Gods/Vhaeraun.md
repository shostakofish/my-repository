_The Masked Lord, Shadow of the Drow, Patron of the Male Rebellion_

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Symbol:** A black half-mask or a dagger silhouetted against a moon
- **Domain:** Trickery, Twilight, War
- **Pantheon:** Dark Seldarine (Faerûnian Drow Pantheon)
- **Worshipers:** Male drow, rebels, assassins, surface-dwellers, outcasts 

### Overview

**Vhaeraun** is the drow god of stealth, subterfuge, rebellion, and the male drow's fight for equality under the matriarchal rule of Lolth. While most surface-dwellers view the drow as a monolithic force of cruelty and darkness, Vhaeraun represents a schism—a vision of independence from Lolth's tyranny and a future in which the drow reclaim the surface world in stealth and shadow.  
He encourages ambition, cunning, and personal freedom. His faithful work to undermine the dominance of Lolth, dismantle the yoke of the Underdark priestesses, and return the drow to a more balanced, surface-dwelling society. Vhaeraun values secrecy and subtlety as much as action; his path is one of masks and blades, not loud revolt.
 
### Tenets of Vhaeraun

- **Reclaim the Surface:** The Underdark is a prison. The surface is the rightful domain of the drow.
- **Equality Through Cunning:** The matriarchy is not ordained—it is imposed. All drow deserve equal voice, and power must be seized in silence.
- **The Masked Path:** Never show your true intentions. Disguise is strength, and deception is survival. 
### Myths and Lore

- **The First Mask:** Vhaeraun forged a mask from shadow and silver moonlight to walk unseen among the spider-priestesses of Lolth. With it, he whispered rebellion into the hearts of his followers.
- **The Blade Beneath the Throne:** In one myth, Vhaeraun infiltrated a Lolthite temple and left a dagger on the High Priestess's pillow without being seen. The message was clear: power can be taken at any moment.
- **The Eclipse War:** Vhaeraun once eclipsed the moon to hide an army of surface drow crossing the mountains under cover of night. Though the battle was lost, the act became legend among his faithful, a symbol of bold defiance. 
### Clergy and Temples

Vhaeraun's clergy are typically male drow, often exiles or secret adherents within Lolthite cities. They wear sleek black leathers and silver masks, often leading double lives as artisans, assassins, or smugglers.  
Temples to Vhaeraun are hidden sanctuaries, carved into stone or forest, lit only by moonlight or illusion. Offerings include stolen relics, masks, and blades dipped in poison. Rituals are performed in silence, marked by gestures and the drawing of symbolic shadow-lines.
 
### Relationships

Vhaeraun despises Lolth above all, viewing her as the root of drow suffering. He is rivals with her other children and allies only with those who value personal freedom, such as Erevan Ilesere or Mask. Surface gods rarely acknowledge him, but some see the potential for reform through his cause.

### Signs of Worship

- Masks hidden in unexpected places
- Black-bladed daggers left as warnings
- Moonlight falling in strange patterns indoors
- Murals or graffiti of hooded figures watching from shadows

## Temple of Vaeraun
_Sanctum of the Masked Lord, Shrine of Silent Blades_  
**Location Description:**  
This clandestine temple is concealed within a crevasse above an ancient Underdark thoroughfare, its entrance veiled behind a permanent illusion of jagged stone. Inside, the walls are polished obsidian, reflecting torchlight like black mirrors, and the air smells faintly of oiled leather and smoke. Silence dominates the space—footsteps echo only once, and whispered prayers are swallowed by enchantments. Shadowy alcoves house blade altars and stolen relics, while a central dais is marked with Vhaeraun’s mask symbol in silver, flanked by two black-bladed swords. The whole temple feels like a den of secrets, ever watchful for betrayal or opportunity.  
**Flavor Text (for players):**  
You pass through an illusory wall into a chamber shrouded in darkness and gleaming with hidden menace. Twin black swords rest upon a silver-marked altar, and the stone beneath your feet drinks in your footsteps. You have entered a place where stealth is sacred, and every shadow hides both worship and judgment.
 
### Rituals & Offerings

Followers of Vhaeraun conduct rituals in silence or hushed murmurs, accompanied by shadowplay—ritual dances of sword and movement meant to honor stealth and guile. Offerings include blades that have drawn blood in the name of freedom or vengeance, as well as trophies stolen from surface cities or rival priesthoods. Devotees wear black silk veils or silver masks during worship, and some high rituals involve casting shadows over bright objects to “blind the tyranny of light.”
 
### NPCs of the Temple

**Whispershade Veldrin Olonrae** _(Drow)_  
A slender drow male dressed in form-fitting black leather armor etched with runes of silence and warding; his eyes are obscured by a silver half-mask.

- _Goals:_ Undermine the priestesses of Lolth and reclaim surface freedom
- _Loves:_ Strategy, stolen relics, the thrill of rebellion
- _Hates:_ Matriarchal dominance, rigid hierarchy
- _Fears:_ Capture by Lolth’s agents, losing the faith of his followers 
**Blade-Kin Zhantra Melarn** _(Half-Drow)_  
Wearing a crimson hood over one side of her face and bearing a magical tattoo that shifts like smoke, she dual-wields daggers infused with surface moonlight.

- _Goals:_ Prove herself worthy as a leader of outcasts
- _Loves:_ Surface air, twin duels, loyal betrayal
- _Hates:_ Elven purists, blind loyalty
- _Fears:_ Being cast out from both surface and Underdark societies 
### Magical Effects

- **Shadowbind Sanctuary:** Light sources within the temple dim to a 5-foot radius unless wielded by a follower of Vhaeraun.
- **Veil of Truths:** Any lie told within the temple's central sanctum is marked by a brief, painful shock (1 psychic damage), discouraging falsehoods in sacred matters.
- **Mask of Escape:** Once per long rest, any follower can _Misty Step_ or _Invisibility_ while touching the central altar. 
### Environmental Effects (Optional Additions)

**Positive Effects (for allies or Vhaeraun's faithful):**

- **Blessing of Silence:** Allies gain +5 to Stealth checks within the temple’s walls.
- **Watcher's Favor:** While in the temple, passive Perception increases by 2 for followers of Vhaeraun.
- **Dagger’s Boon:** The first attack made from hiding each day deals an additional 1d4 psychic damage.

**Negative Effects (for outsiders or enemies):**

- **False Paths:** Non-worshipers see altered illusions of doors and halls (as per _Hallucinatory Terrain_), leading them in circles.
- **Shadow Lash:** Shadows in the corners may animate and lash out if any sacred object is touched without permission (as per _Arms of Hadar_).
- **Echo of Betrayal:** Characters who have betrayed a companion within the last 24 hours have disadvantage on all saving throws while inside.
