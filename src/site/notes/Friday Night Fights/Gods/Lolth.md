**Lolth**  
_Queen of the Demonweb Pits, The Spider Queen, Weaver of Shadows_

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Symbol:** A spider with the head of a female drow
- **Domain:** Trickery, War
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian (Drow Pantheon)
- **Worshipers:** Drow, assassins, spies, priestesses of power 
### Overview

Lolth is the capricious and cruel goddess of the drow, revered as both a tyrant and a savior in the Underdark. Ruling from the Demonweb Pits, a plane of her own making in the Abyss, Lolth weaves lies and fate like threads in her web, pulling her followers into cycles of betrayal and ambition. She is both worshiped and feared, and those who displease her often meet slow, excruciating ends.  
Her power is absolute among traditional drow societies, where her priestesses reign supreme and her dogma justifies both conquest and cruelty. Lolth demands strength through chaos, and favors the cunning, the ambitious, and the ruthless. She thrives on deception, encouraging her followers to undermine each other to gain her favor.
 
### Tenets of Lolth

- **Fear is Power:** Rule through terror, and do not show weakness.
- **Deception is a Sacred Tool:** Lies are a weapon, wielded best by those who smile as they strike.
- **Only the Strong Deserve Favor:** Prove your worth through cunning, cruelty, and dominance. 
### Myths and Lore

- **The Web of Betrayal:** Lolth once wove a grand planar web that snared the essence of a rival demon lord, consuming him and twisting his power into her own, demonstrating that betrayal is the highest form of strength.
- **The Fall of Araushnee:** Once a goddess of destiny and artistry among the Seldarine, Lolth's transformation into a demon goddess came after she betrayed Corellon Larethian, her former lover, and attempted to usurp the heavens. Cast down, she embraced her monstrous form and found new power in her hatred.
- **The Poisoned Pact:** Lolth granted a drow city great wealth and protection in exchange for a single, secret sacrifice: the unborn child of its high priestess. The pact was honored, and the city prospered—until the secret was discovered and civil war tore the city apart, pleasing Lolth immensely. 
### Clergy and Temples

Lolth’s priesthood is exclusively female, with priestesses commanding absolute authority in most drow settlements. Temples to Lolth are often built in caverns lit by phosphorescent fungus and adorned with obsidian statues of spiders. Blood offerings, poisonous rituals, and acts of dominance are common in worship, and every high-ranking priestess vies for influence by undermining her rivals.  
Acolytes are trained in the arts of poison, seduction, shadow magic, and interrogation. Outsiders—especially males—rarely survive long in temples unless kept as thralls or sacrifices.
 
### Relationships

Lolth is reviled by the surface deities, especially the Seldarine, who view her as a traitor and blasphemer. She opposes **Eilistraee**, her daughter, whose goals of redemption and peace among the drow directly contradict Lolth’s ideals. She also competes with **Vhaeraun**, her son, whose ambitions for male equality and stealthy domination undermine her matriarchal rule.  
Among demon lords, she tolerates alliances with beings like **Graz’zt** or **Zuggtmoy** when it suits her web of schemes—but trusts none.
 
### Signs of Worship

- Spiderweb motifs woven into clothing, tapestries, or tattoos.
- Statues of eight-legged women in seductive or predatory poses.
- Sudden disappearances of rivals or public executions justified as “sacrifices.”
- Rare albino spiders used as omens or familiars.

## Temple of Lloth
_Shrine of the Web Queen, Vault of Endless Schemes_  
**Location Description:**  
Hidden deep within the cavernous Underdark, the Temple of Lolth clings to the ceiling of a vast, lightless chasm. Thick webs stretch across the expanse, shimmering faintly with unnatural phosphorescence, while the sound of chittering spiders echoes in the vast gloom. Faint hints of blood and perfume cling to the air, a paradox of reverence and slaughter. Black stone arches rise like spider legs over narrow bridges, and obsidian altars are veined with violet gems that pulse faintly when sacrifices are made. A ceiling-high statue of Lolth, half-drow and half-spider, watches with jeweled eyes that follow intruders no matter their path. The temple itself is alive with whispered intrigue, echoing prayers, and the skittering movement of unseen watchers.  
**Flavor Text (for players):**  
As your eyes adjust to the pitch-black chamber, a lattice of shimmering webs reveals itself, stretching across a yawning void. Suspended in the center is a shrine wrought from obsidian and bone, lit only by a sickly violet glow from within the walls. Eight carved spider legs reach toward you from the darkness, framing a statue whose smile is beautiful, cruel, and eternal.
 
### Rituals & Offerings

Worshipers of Lolth offer living sacrifices—often drow or surface-dwellers deemed unworthy—bound and pierced upon the altar. Rituals take place during _Unlight_, when no faerzress glows, and involve bloodletting, chants in Deep Drow, and weaving intricate webs across the temple to represent cycles of betrayal and rebirth. A web-drum called the _Vraklor_ is used in sacred ceremonies, played by striking tight webbing stretched across bone frames.
 
### NPCs of the Temple

- **High Priestess Zilvra T’sarran** _(Drow)_  
    A statuesque drow clad in a flowing black silken robe woven with tiny spiders that live within its folds; her silver hair is intricately braided into a crown of thorns.
- _Goals:_ Secure her matron house’s dominance; become Lolth’s voice in the material plane
- _Loves:_ Power gained through treachery, watching rivals suffer
- _Hates:_ Weakness, surface elves, anyone who defies Lolth
- _Fears:_ Losing Lolth’s favor, public humiliation
- **The Tormentor-Midwife Thruzla** _(Drider)_  
    Twisted and regal, her upper body resembles a heavily tattooed drow midwife, her spider abdomen hung with bells that chime discordantly.
- _Goals:_ Cultivate pain as religious ecstasy
- _Loves:_ Screams, rituals gone "wrong," turning faith into fanaticism
- _Hates:_ Mercy, surface clerics, her former self
- _Fears:_ Redemption, light, spiders turning on her 
### Magical Effects

- **Sanctified Shadows:** Within the inner sanctum, magical darkness acts as _true sight_ for Lolth’s faithful but blinds all others.
- **Web of Secrets:** Whispers spoken anywhere in the temple are gathered by spider familiars and delivered to the High Priestess at nightfall.
- **Curse of the Betrayer:** Anyone who enters the temple after betraying a drow noble suffers _disadvantage on all Charisma checks_ while inside, and the eyes of every spider seem to watch them. 
### Environmental Effects (Optional Additions)

**Positive Effects (for allies or Lolth's chosen):**

- **Webstep Passage:** Spider webs act as _Misty Step_ portals for the faithful, allowing short-range teleportation.
- **Eyes of the Weaver:** Once per day, a follower may cast _Clairvoyance_ through one of the temple’s carved spider eyes.
- **Blessing of Shadow:** Allies of Lolth gain advantage on Stealth checks in the temple.

**Negative Effects (for outsiders or enemies):**

- **Chittering Madness:** Whispers from the web inflict _confusion_ (DC 15 Wisdom save) on intruders who sleep within the temple.
- **Blood-Warped Stone:** The blackstone alters healing magic; any healing spell cast here heals only half as much unless the caster is a follower of Lolth.
- **Living Webs:** Webs animate with _animate object_, entangling enemies and attempting to strangle them when rituals are disturbed.
