_Our Lady of Joy, Mistress of Revels, the Everchanging Goddess_


- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Symbol:** Triangle of three six-pointed stars (often in bright colors)
- **Domain:** Life, Light, Trickery
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Worshipers:** Entertainers, dancers, revelers, artists, free spirits, festival-goers, hedonists
 
## **Overview**

Lliira is the goddess of joy, celebration, freedom, and uninhibited expression. She embodies the belief that life is fleeting and should be filled with laughter, dance, and shared happiness. Where sorrow and hardship linger, Lliira’s influence brings music, color, and the promise that even the darkest times can be lifted—if only for a night.  
To her followers, joy is not frivolous—it is sacred. Lliira teaches that celebration builds community, heals wounds of the spirit, and defies despair. Her faith thrives in festivals, parades, and spontaneous gatherings, where structure gives way to freedom and strangers become companions.  
Though seemingly carefree, Lliira is not blind to suffering. She stands in quiet opposition to tyranny, cruelty, and oppression, believing that joy itself is an act of rebellion against those who would control or diminish others.
 
## **Tenets of Lliira**

- **Spread Joy Freely:** Bring laughter, music, and light wherever darkness lingers.
- **Celebrate Life:** Every moment is worth honoring—through dance, art, or companionship.
- **Break Chains with Laughter:** Oppose oppression not only with force, but with defiant joy.
- **Embrace Freedom:** Restriction of harmless expression is an affront to the spirit.
 
## **Myths and Lore**

- **The Dance of Endless Delight:** It is said Lliira once danced for seven days and nights to drive back a creeping despair that threatened a great city, her movements turning grief into celebration.
- **The Ribbon of Rebirth:** Legends tell of Lliira gifting a grieving widow a rainbow ribbon, which transformed sorrow into song each time it was worn, reminding all that joy can follow loss.
- **The Festival Eternal:** Some believe that in her divine realm, a celebration never ends—a place where all are welcome, and no soul knows loneliness or despair.
 
## **Clergy and Temples**

Temples to Lliira are vibrant, open spaces filled with colorful banners, ribbons, lanterns, and music. Many are open-air courtyards or festival grounds rather than enclosed sanctuaries. Even permanent temples feel temporary—designed to host gatherings rather than solemn rituals.  
Clerics of Lliira, often called Joybringers, wear bright, flowing garments adorned with bells, scarves, and painted symbols. They act as performers, healers of the spirit, and organizers of festivals, traveling from place to place spreading celebration.  
Rituals are spontaneous and communal—feasts, dances, storytelling, and shared drink. Even funerals under Lliira’s guidance become celebrations of life, honoring the departed with laughter as much as tears.
 
## **Relationships**

Lliira shares close ties with deities of beauty, art, and love such as Sune and Sharess, often appearing alongside them in festivals and celebrations.  
She is friendly with Tymora, embracing the spontaneity and unpredictability both embody.  
In contrast, she opposes oppressive and controlling deities such as Bane, whose rigid dominance stifles the freedom she cherishes.  
Even somber gods like Kelemvor are treated with respectful distance—Lliira does not deny death, but insists life before it should be vibrant.
 
## **Signs of Worship**

- Bright ribbons, streamers, and colorful banners in public spaces
- Spontaneous dancing, music, and laughter breaking out in crowds
- Painted symbols of stars or triangles in vivid hues
- Shared drinks, feasts, and communal celebrations
- Bells, drums, and rhythmic clapping echoing through gatherings
 
## **Earning and Losing Favor**

### **Actions That Gain Favor**

Bring joy to a community in despair.  
Organize or lead a celebration, festival, or gathering.  
Lift someone from grief through kindness, humor, or art.  
Defy oppression through peaceful or joyful expression.  
Create art, music, or performance that inspires others.  
Encourage unity among strangers through shared experience.  
Protect places or people dedicated to joy and freedom.  
Celebrate life even in the face of danger or hardship.
 
### **Actions That Lose Favor**

Suppress harmless joy, art, or celebration.  
Spread misery, despair, or needless cruelty.  
Exploit others under the guise of pleasure or revelry.  
Break trust during communal gatherings or festivals.  
Enforce rigid control that stifles freedom of expression.  
Turn celebration into harm, excess, or destruction.  
Mock genuine joy or corrupt it for selfish ends.  
Refuse connection, isolating others from shared happiness.
  
## **Boons and Blessings**

Lliira’s blessings are vibrant, fleeting, and often shared—meant to uplift not just the faithful, but those around them.

- **Joyful Resilience:** Once per long rest, when reduced to 0 hit points, you instead drop to 1 hit point and immediately gain advantage on your next roll as laughter or music fills your senses.
- **Aura of Celebration:** As an action, you can inspire those around you; allies within 10 feet gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.
- **Dancer’s Grace:** You gain advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks and saving throws made to avoid being restrained or grappled.
- **Festival’s Favor:** During a social gathering or celebration, you have advantage on Charisma checks, and hostile creatures are less likely to initiate violence without cause.
- **Song of Renewal:** After performing music, dance, or storytelling for at least 1 minute, you and up to six creatures regain hit points equal to 1d6 + your proficiency bonus (once per long rest).
- **Defiant Joy:** You have advantage on saving throws against fear and charm effects that would suppress your emotions or autonomy.
 
## **Banes and Curses**

Those who betray Lliira’s ideals find joy slipping from their grasp, replaced by emptiness and isolation.

- **Hollow Laughter:** You suffer disadvantage on Charisma (Performance) and (Persuasion) checks, your attempts at joy feeling forced and unsettling.
- **Joyless Spirit:** You cannot gain inspiration, and effects that grant temporary hit points are reduced by half.
- **Weight of Misery:** You have disadvantage on saving throws against fear and despair-based effects.
- **Festival’s Rejection:** In social gatherings, creatures are subtly uncomfortable around you; friendly attitudes are harder to maintain without effort.
- **Fractured Connection:** You cannot benefit from Help actions unless you succeed on a DC 12 Charisma check to meaningfully engage with the ally.
- **Echo of Silence:** Music sounds dull, colors seem muted, and any attempt to create art or celebration feels empty—imposing disadvantage on related checks.
