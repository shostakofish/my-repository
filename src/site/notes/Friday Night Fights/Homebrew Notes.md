##### Items
Ring of Late Nights Wondrous item, Uncommon (requires attunement) A white ivory band with inlaid opal gemstone. While attuned to this ring you no longer gain Exhaustion from not sleeping, but you automatically fail any saving throws against resisting effects that would put you to sleep. (For the spell Sleep, you are considered to have 1 hitpoint.) 

Ring of the Lifesaver Wondrous item, Uncommon A red and white striped ring wrapped in white strands of rope. As an action you can speak the ring’s command word to inflate the ring to a 5ft diameter lifebuoy. Speaking the command word again will return it to its ring form 

Boomering Wondrous item, Uncommon Once per day as an action, you can throw any object and have it magically return to your hands. A Dexterity saving throw must be made to catch the item without incident DC 10. 

Ring of Explosion Wondrous item, Uncommon A large ruby is inset on a thin silver band. As an action you can activate this ring and in 1d4 rounds it will explode and cast the spell fireball originating on itself; its spell save DC is 18. This destroys the ring.
